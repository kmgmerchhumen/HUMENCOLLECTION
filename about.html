ABOUT HUMEN

Dr. Kevin “Khao” Cates • Founder

Kevin “Khao” Cates is a Grammy-nominated music producer, entrepreneur, speaker, and philanthropist, renowned for his contributions to the music industry and his commitment to education and social change. He has worked with top artists across genres, producing chart-topping hits, and is known for his innovative approach to blending music with education.

As a sought-after speaker, Khao shares his insights on entrepreneurship, creativity, and community impact, inspiring audiences worldwide. Through his initiatives like Bridge DA GAP and KOOLriculum, Khao leverages his expertise to create platforms that teach social, emotional, and academic skills to youth.

His work extends into film production with KMG Studios, logistics, and a variety of business portfolios, further showcasing his diverse talents and dedication to empowering future generations.

“Don’t just be successful. Be significant.”

DR. KEVIN “KHAO” CATES

WHAT IS HUMEN?

HUMEN is a comprehensive personal development platform designed to help men navigate life’s challenges, break free from societal pressures, and unlock their full potential. Through a powerful combination of books, journals, card games, video affirmations, and online courses, HUMEN offers practical tools and strategies to enhance emotional, mental, and spiritual well-being.

Whether you’re looking to build resilience, improve relationships, or find purpose, HUMEN provides real solutions for real men.

HUMEN Collection is the wearable extension of this movement — every design is crafted with intention, connected to a song, a lesson, or a truth from the journey of becoming your best self. This isn’t just clothing. It’s a statement. It’s armor. It’s identity.
